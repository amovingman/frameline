# Research brief: editing workspace

- **User:** video editors reviewing project revisions and incoming clip changes.
- **Job:** see what changed, identify conflicts, and choose whether a pull updates the current file or creates a review copy.
- **Primary surface:** desktop app with pointer and keyboard, used during focused editing sessions.
- **Constraints:** editor adapter is not implemented; all revision content must be marked as sample data. File selection can work, but no claim of parsing or pulling.
- **Feel:** precise, calm, production oriented. The timeline should be the main visual anchor.
- **Success:** users can distinguish incoming changes, conflict status, and the two pull modes at a glance.
